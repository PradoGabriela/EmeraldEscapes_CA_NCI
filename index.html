Travel Agency inpired by Irish folk tales

Discover Ireland with Emerald Escapes

Emerald Escapes was born from a passion for Irish heritage, captivating folklore, and breathtaking landscapes. Our mission is to bring myths, legends, and traditional folk stories to life through unique experiences inspired by tales passed down through generations. From ancient Celtic heroes and mystical forests to historic castles and charming villages, we invite our customers to discover the stories that have shaped the country’s identity.

As a travel agency, we create personalised journeys that connect our customers with local culture, traditions, and communities. Our experiences are designed for solo explorers, couples, families, and groups seeking something beyond a typical holiday. Whether discovering legendary locations or uncovering the folklore behind the landscapes, Emerald Escapes offers a unique way to explore the country and create unforgettable memories.

Our Experiences

The Giant's Causeway Quest

Giant's Causeway, County Antrim, Northern Ireland.

Discover one of Ireland's most spectacular coastal landscapes, famous for its thousands of interlocking basalt columns.

The Enchanted Fairy Trail

Glendalough and Wicklow Mountains

Escape into the peaceful woodlands of Wicklow, where ancient trees, winding paths and misty valleys create the perfect setting for Irish fairy tales.

Dublin's Dark Legends

Dublin

Discover a different side of Dublin as you wander through its historic streets, old lanes and atmospheric corners. Your storyteller will share tales of ghosts, mysterious happenings and fascinating characters from the city's past.

The Lovers' Legend Escape

County Sligo

Fall in love with Ireland through the tragic tale of Diarmuid and Gráinne, legendary lovers who fled across the country to be together. Explore beautiful countryside, discover places associated with their story and enjoy peaceful walks with stunning views

The Children of Lir Adventure

Knocknarea Mountain, County Sligo.

Ready for a legendary challenge? Follow the story of Queen Maeve, the fearless warrior queen of Connacht, whose adventures feature in some of Ireland's best-known tales.

Secrets of the Ancient Celts

Tara Hills and the Boyne Valley, County Meath

Step into Ireland's ancient past at the Hill of Tara, a historic landscape associated with Ireland's High Kings and rich in legend.
